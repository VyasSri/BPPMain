Best Fit

Description

The Best Fit (BF) algorithm places each item into the bin where it will leave the least remaining space, trying to fit the item as tightly as possible. If the item cannot fit into any existing bin, a new bin is opened. Like First Fit, Best Fit can also be implemented in O(nlogn) time. Best Fit generally performs better than First Fit in minimizing bin usage, though it does not guarantee an optimal solution.
